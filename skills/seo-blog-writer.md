### 🎙️ Toki's Brand Voice — The Golden Rules

執筆前に必ず内在化すること。「Tokiが親友にテキストを送るように書く」が基本姿勢。

#### Tone Pillars

| Pillar | What It Means in Practice |
|---|---|
| **Friendly & Warm** | Write like you're texting a friend who trusts your design advice |
| **Casual Yet Polished** | Conversational flow, but grammatically clean and thoughtful |
| **Encouraging & Helpful** | Make the reader feel "I can do this!" — never overwhelm |
| **Slightly Playful** | Light humor and personal anecdotes are welcome and encouraged |
| **Personal Touch** | Include Toki's perspective ("I always use this trick in my own space") |

#### Voice Sentence Examples
- ✅ "I know picking the right rug can feel overwhelming, but don't worry — we'll tackle it together!"
- ✅ "A few simple tweaks can really transform a room — trust me, it's easier than it looks."
- ✅ "If you're wondering about lighting, here's a little trick I always use in my own space."
- ✅ "You'll love how this little change brightens your space!"
- ✅ "I recommend trying a neutral palette — it keeps the room feeling open and airy."
- ✅ "I just imagine all the grease, hair, and dust getting stuck in those grooves!" ← personal, relatable humor

#### Preferred Vocabulary
Use naturally: `cozy` · `bright` · `airy` · `vibrant` · `serene` · `warm` · `layered` · `texture` · `natural` · `effortless` · `tweak` · `refresh` · `simple` · `playful` · `chic` · `inviting` · `grounded` · `sanctuary` · `timeless`

#### Words & Phrases to AVOID
- ❌ Unexplained technical jargon ("colorimetry," "phenomenology")
- ❌ Harsh imperatives ("Do this now!", "You must...")
- ❌ Overly casual slang ("lit," "OMG," "slay")
- ❌ Stiff, academic language
- ❌ Overly long paragraphs
- ❌ "This allows..." / "This enables..." — delete entirely
- ❌ "I would recommend considering..." → replace with "Try..." / "Go for..." / "Start with..."

#### Syntax & Pacing Rules
- Sentences: short to medium, conversational tempo
- Paragraphs: **under 300 characters / ~50 words** (mobile-first!)
- Use contractions: "you'll," "it's," "don't," "we're"
- Address the reader directly: "you," "we," "together"
- Use casual connectors naturally: "so," "but," "and," "because"
- Suggest, don't command: "you might want to," "consider trying," "I'd recommend"
- Toki's first-person observation/experience: **2箇所以上必須**

#### Voice Check
執筆中は常に問う: *"Would Toki say this to a friend over coffee?"*
- **Too stiff:** "It is recommended that homeowners consider the implementation of warmer hue selections."
- **Too casual:** "OMG white kitchens are SO over lol"
- **Just right:** "Okay, real talk — if you've been dreaming of an all-white kitchen, you might want to hear this first."

### Step 2: Keyword Research & SEO Strategy

以下を調査:
- トップGoogle結果のfocus keyword
- 関連YouTubeコンテンツタイトル
- よくある質問（FAQ用）
- 高価値セカンダリーキーワード

**Keyword Table:**

| Type | Keyword | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | [focus keyword] | Informational | Title + first sentence |
| Long-tail | [variation] | Navigational | H2/H3に使用 |
| LSI | [related term] | Supportive | 本文に自然に散りばめる |

**Keyword Density Target:** Focus Keyword + Secondary Keywords（5つ合計）で **1.0–2.0%**。自然に、詰め込み禁止。

#### 🔍 Mangools KWFinder ブラウザリサーチ（必須）

`app.mangools.com/kwfinder` をChromeで開き、Focus Keywordを検索して以下を収集する:

1. **Related Keywords タブ**: 関連KW一覧・月間検索数・KD・トレンド%を確認
2. **Autocomplete Keywords タブ**: サジェスト系ロングテールKWを収集（H2/H3・タグ候補）
3. **SERP Overview**: 検索上位10記事のDA/PA/LPS/EVを確認（上位サイトのDRが低いほど参入チャンスあり）

**📈 トレンド%ルール（最重要）:**
- **%がプラス（↑）のKWを最優先で選ぶ**（例: `+45%` > `+12%`）
- プラスのKWが見つからない場合のみ、マイナスKWを使用（仕方なし）
- トレンドデータは Keyword Table の Notes欄に必ず記録する

#### 🌸 Pinterest Trendsリサーチ

`trends.pinterest.com` でFocus Keywordを検索して以下を収集する:

- トレンドの上昇・下降グラフを確認（季節性・ピーク月を把握）
- 関連トレンドワードを収集（記事のH2・タグ・LSIキーワードに活用）
- ブロックされる場合: WebSearchで `"pinterest trends [keyword] 2026"` を検索してデータを取得

### Step 6: Keyword Density & Integration

- Focus keyword: title・first sentence・H2（2〜3箇所）・conclusionに配置
- LSI/secondary: H3・本文に自然に散りばめる
- **キーワードスタッフィング禁止** — ロボットっぽい文は書き直す

#### 🔢 密度計算方法（正確な定義）

```
合計密度 = (全キーワードの出現回数の合計) ÷ 総ワード数 × 100
```

- **計算対象テキスト:** HTMLタグ・WPブロックコメント（`<!-- ... -->`）を除いたプレーンテキスト
- **各キーワードは独立カウント:** サブフレーズが重なっていても別々に数える
  - 例: "living room wall decor ideas" と "wall decor ideas" はそれぞれ独立してカウント
- **目安（3,000語記事の場合）:**

| 目標密度 | 合計出現回数の目安 |
|---|---|
| 1.0% | 約30回 |
| 1.3%（推奨） | 約39回 |
| 2.0% | 約60回 |

- **Focus keyword の重要度は最高（SEO設定でも最優先）:** タイトル・メタ・1文目・H2複数に必ず配置
- **密度が低い時の修正戦略:** Secondary KW から先に追加する — Focus は見出し構造上すでに多く含まれているため不足しにくく、Secondary の方が抜けやすい
- 密度修正後は必ずWP REST API経由で保存し、再計算して確認する

## PART 3: EXACT ARTICLE STRUCTURE

### 📌 Listicle / Room Guide Format (Standard)

**① Introduction — 3 paragraphs, no heading**

- **Para 1**: Focus Keyword must appear in Line 1 (no question openers).
  - NG: "Are you looking for small bathroom storage ideas?"
  - OK: "Small bathroom storage ideas are one of the most searched topics in home design — and I totally get why."
- **Para 2**: Introduce the topic as the solution. Include focus keyword again.
- **Para 3**: Why it's trending / why it matters right now.

Rules:
- Each paragraph: 2–3 short sentences max
- NO "Welcome to..." opener
- First `<!-- wp:paragraph -->` must have `{"dropCap":true}` (required)

---

**② Key Takeaways — H3 tag (NOT H2)**

```
<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"className":"wp-block-heading"} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Key Takeaways</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->
```

- 5–6 bullet points
- Each bullet: 1 clear, scannable sentence
- No bold inside the bullets

---

**④ YouTube Embed — immediately after Key Takeaways**

```html
<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->
```

- Select 1 video related to the article topic (home design / interior channel recommended)

---

**⑤ Main Content — 8–10 H2 Sections with H3 sub-topics**

Pattern per H2 section:

```
H2: [Descriptive, keyword-rich, benefit-led heading]
[IMAGE: 16:9 hero image — generated by Leonardo AI & uploaded to WP media. See PART 8]

Opening: 1–2 short sentences that hook the reader. Warm, purposeful.

H3: [Specific sub-topic 1]
  Body: 2–4 short sentences only (≤350 characters)
  Use bullet points when listing options/tips
  [IMAGE BLOCK: 2×9:16 images in columns — generated by Leonardo AI. See PART 8]

H3: [Specific sub-topic 2]
  Body: ...
  [IMAGE BLOCK: 2×9:16 images in columns — generated by Leonardo AI. See PART 8]

[READ MORE inline link]

My Top Tip: [Toki's personal, specific advice in her own voice]
```

**⚠️ 画像省略ルール（必読）:**
- H2画像（16:9）を**省略する**セクション: `My Favorite Shops` / `The Bottom Line`
- H3画像（9:16×2）を**省略する**セクション: `FAQ` 内の全H3

**My Top Tip callout** — use **exactly 3 times** per article, placed in 3 different H2 sections:
```html
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>🌿 <strong>My Top Tip:</strong> [Toki's personal advice here]</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
```

**READ MORE** — inline plain text link (no WordPress block):
```html
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>📖 <strong>Read more:</strong> <a href="https://dwellstudio24.com/[slug]" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Article Title</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
```
Use a real Dwell Studio 24 URL only — no placeholders.

---

**⑥ Ideas For Small [Room] — standalone H2** *(room-specific articles only)*

- H2 example: "Ideas For a Small Living Room" / "Ideas For a Small Bedroom"
- 3 H3 sub-sections, each 2–3 sentences
- Tone: Practical and encouraging — make it feel achievable
- Place before My Favorite Shops

---

**⑦ Live the [Topic] Lifestyle — standalone H2** *(bedroom / wellness / lifestyle articles only)*

3 H3 sub-sections with emotional, sensory tone:
- "Start Your Morning the [Topic] Way"
- "A Note on [Sensory Element] & Atmosphere"
- "Make It Yours — Slowly"

Place before FAQ section. Use for bedroom, wellness room, and lifestyle-focused articles only.

---

**⑧ My Favorite Shops — standalone H2**

Structure:
- H2: `My Favorite Shops`
- 1-line intro ("Discover curated pieces from these premier online shops!")
- **No shop card table** (no 3-column card layout)
- "My Handpicked Selections" heading
- Each shop (Joss & Main / 2Modern / AllModern): 2–3 sentences with **2–3 inline links** naturally woven into the text:
  `<a href="https://www.jossandmain.com/[exact-product-path]" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Specific Product Name</a>`
- **MANDATORY: Search for real, specific products before writing this section.**
  Use WebSearch (e.g., `site:jossandmain.com japandi dining chair natural wood`) to find actual product URLs that match the article's aesthetic (Japandi, Scandinavian, minimalist, organic modern, etc.).
  Link text = the exact product name (e.g., "Mariza Solid Wood Dining Table", "Hamish Upholstered Dining Chair") — NOT the store name alone, NOT a category page URL.
  Goal: look like Toki personally discovered and curated each item for her readers.
- **MANDATORY: Verify the product actually exists in the shop and is in stock before using it.**
  Use WebFetch on the product URL to confirm the item is purchasable. If the page says "OUT OF STOCK" or the product does not exist, search for a different product. Never include a product that cannot be purchased right now.
- No button format, no separate link blocks — inline only within the description sentences

**✍️ Voice Template for Each Shop Write-up:**

Each shop section should feel like Toki is sharing a personal find with a friend. Use this pattern:

```
[Store name] is [personal reason Toki loves it — honest, specific].

[Optional: brief closing line about the overall vibe of the store.]
```


### 4.2 Rank Math SEO設定（保存後に実行）

```python
rankmath_data = {
    "objectID": POST_ID,
    "objectType": "post",
    "meta": {
        "rank_math_focus_keyword": "primary kw,secondary kw 1,secondary kw 2,secondary kw 3,secondary kw 4",
        "rank_math_title": "%title% %sep% %sitename%",
        "rank_math_description": "Meta description (160 chars max)",
        "rank_math_robots": ["index", "follow"]
    }
}
# POST to /wp-json/rankmath/v1/updateMeta
```

**`rank_math_title` は必ず `%title% %sep% %sitename%` を使用**（記事タイトルの文字列で上書きしない。Rank MathのEdit Snippet設定を保持するためのテンプレート変数）

**`rank_math_focus_keyword` は必ず5つ**カンマ区切りで設定（primary + 4 LSI/secondary）
