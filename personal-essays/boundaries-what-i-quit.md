# What I Quit

後から考えて嫌だなという気持ちになったり愚痴になるような頼まれことは無理して引き受けないこと。

---

## The Insight

自分の心が「嫌だ」と言っているのに、義務感で「はい」と返事をしたら：
- あとで後悔する
- 愚痴が増える
- 心が疲れる
- その関係も壊れやすくなる

**それなら最初から「できません」と言う方が、自分にも相手にも誠実。**

---

## For Skin Essays

This connects to skincare & wellness philosophy:

- **Skincare is about saying NO to what doesn't serve you**
  - Bad ingredients
  - Products that irritate
  - Trends that don't fit your skin
  
- **The same principle applies to life**
  - No to requests that drain you
  - No to people-pleasing that costs your peace
  - No to anything that becomes "grumbling" later

- **Boundaries = Self-Care**
  - It's not selfish; it's the foundation of everything else
  - You can only be present (like at the beach) when you're not carrying resentment
  - Clean slate. Clear choices. Peace.

---

## Reminders

- Trust your gut when it says "嫌だ"
- A polite No now > frustrated Yes later
- Your boundaries protect your serenity
- This is part of "the richness of nothing" — nothing forced, nothing resentful
